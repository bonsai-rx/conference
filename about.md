The Bonsai Developer Conference is a three-day conference aimed at bringing together neuroscience researchers, computational scientists, and software engineers who are interested in developing and using the [Bonsai](https://bonsai-rx.org/) visual reactive programming language.

It will be held in Lisbon, Portugal from **Monday, 7 December 2026 to Wednesday, 9 December 2026** at the Teaching Lab of the [Champalimaud Centre for the Unknown](https://www.fchampalimaud.org/).

Organizers

- [Gonçalo Lopes](https://github.com/glopesdev), NeuroGEARS
- [João Frazão](https://github.com/jfrazao), NeuroGEARS
- [Hugo Marques](https://www.fchampalimaud.org/people/hugo-marques), Champalimaud Foundation
